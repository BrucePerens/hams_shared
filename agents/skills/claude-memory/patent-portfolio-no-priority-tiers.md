# Treat every patent disclosure as a real filing; the "conversion priority" tiers are stale

`docs/proposals/PATENT_STRATEGY.md` and `MASTER_CLAIMS.md` in `hams_com` carry
a "conversion priority" ranking (Highest / High / Medium / Defer) that came from
an outside review relaying a Grok-assisted critique in late August 2026. On
2026-09-08 Bruce said to ignore it: "Ignore conversion priority. We are filing
all of them and that came from a Grok analysis that does not include more
recent patents." Two consequences for any future work on the portfolio:

- Do not use those tiers to decide which disclosures get the careful pass and
  which get a lighter one. All 44 active disclosures are being filed and
  deserve the same depth of prior-art search, examiner-style review, and
  verification.
- The tiers also predate disclosures 28-36 entirely. Bruce singled out the
  calibrated-reference dominant-signal attribution family (31, with the
  hearing-protection application in 33 and the array/robot/forensic/UAV
  applications in 32, 34, 35, 36) as possibly the most valuable in the
  portfolio "if we can get it patented", so that family warrants particular
  care in claim drafting and prior-art distinction rather than the "new since
  triage" footnote status the old table gives it.
